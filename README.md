## Stone Bacteria
#### Grow stone instead of mining it!

**Stone Bacteria** expands Gleba's biological production system with a renewable source of stone, just like iron and copper bacteria.

### Recipes

- 🧪 **Create:** 1 Bioflux → 2 Spoilage + 10% chance of 1 Stone Bacteria (1s, Gleba only).
- 🔄 **Multiply:** 1 Stone Bacteria + 1 Bioflux → 4 fresh Stone Bacteria (4s).
- 🪨 **Harvest:** Let your Stone Bacteria spoil naturally into Stone.

### Features

- Both recipes are crafted in **Biochambers**.
- Unlocked through the existing **Bacteria Cultivation** technology.
- No new buildings or technologies required.
- Seamlessly integrated into **Factorio: Space Age**.
