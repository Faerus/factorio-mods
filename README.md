## Stone Bacteria
#### Grow stone instead of mining it!

**Stone Bacteria** expands Gleba's biological production system with a renewable source of stone, just like iron and copper bacteria.

Designed to be **lore-friendly and balanced**, this mod integrates naturally into Factorio: Space Age without disrupting the original gameplay or progression.

### Recipes

- 🧪 **Create:** 1 Bioflux → 2 Spoilage + 10% chance of 1 Stone Bacteria (1s, Gleba only).
- 🔄 **Multiply:** 1 Stone Bacteria + 1 Bioflux → 4 fresh Stone Bacteria (4s).
- 🪨 **Harvest:** Let your Stone Bacteria spoil naturally into Stone.

### Stromatolites

- ⛏️ **Iron and Copper Stromatolites** now yield Stone Bacteria when mined.
- 🪨 Stone production is reduced from **3–7 to 1–3** per stromatolite.
- 🦠 Each stromatolite also provides **1–2 Stone Bacteria**.
- Original iron/copper ore and bacteria yields remain unchanged.

### Features

- 🌿 **Lore-friendly:** Extends Gleba's existing biological mechanics naturally.
- ⚖️ **Balanced:** Renewable stone production requires bioflux, cultivation, and spoilage management.
- Both recipes are crafted in **Biochambers**.
- Unlocked through the existing **Bacteria Cultivation** technology.
- No new buildings or technologies required.
- Seamlessly integrated into **Factorio: Space Age**.
